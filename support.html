Support

Help with HotSwap, Shared Sessions, Live Activities and Apple Watch.

Contact HotSwap Support

For app issues, general feedback, feature requests, privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact:

Email
support.hotswap@gmail.com
Email support

We aim to respond to support requests as soon as reasonably possible.

When reporting a problem

Please include, where relevant:

  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • Apple Watch model and watchOS version
  • HotSwap app version/build
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it helps explain the issue

Shared Sessions

Shared Sessions use Apple iCloud / CloudKit. When creating or joining a session, make sure iCloud is available on the device and that the device has an internet connection.

Live Activities

If a HotSwap Live Activity doesn't appear, check that Live Activities are enabled for HotSwap in iOS Settings. iOS ultimately controls when and where Live Activities are displayed.

Apple Watch

For Apple Watch functionality, keep HotSwap installed on both iPhone and Apple Watch, and keep the devices connected when synchronizing timer changes.

Privacy and data deletion

Read the Privacy Policy or visit Privacy Choices & Data Deletion for information about requesting deletion or withdrawing permissions.
