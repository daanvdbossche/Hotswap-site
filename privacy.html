Privacy Policy

How HotSwap handles information when you use the iPhone and Apple Watch apps.

Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the HotSwap application for iPhone and Apple Watch and to HotSwap support services.

2. Information HotSwap uses

Display name

If you choose a display name, it is used to identify you to other participants in a Shared Session.

Shared Session and timer information

Shared Session functionality may process session names, session participation information, timer states, gear/setup information, Power Groups, battery/runtime information and activity needed to synchronize the session.

Camera access

HotSwap requests camera access only when you choose to scan a Shared Session QR code. Camera access is used for that scanning function.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, HotSwap may deliver timer warnings and Shared Session activity notifications. You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Apple Watch synchronization

Timer and session information may be transferred between your iPhone and paired Apple Watch in order to provide HotSwap Watch features.

3. How information is collected and used

Information is supplied by you when configuring HotSwap or is generated by your use of timers and Shared Sessions. HotSwap uses it only to provide app functionality, synchronize Shared Sessions, identify participants within a session, show timer status, deliver permitted notifications and provide support.

4. Apple iCloud and CloudKit

HotSwap uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure for Shared Sessions and synchronization. Information handled through these services is processed using Apple's infrastructure and is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

HotSwap does not use CloudKit data for advertising.

5. Third parties

HotSwap does not sell personal data and does not use third-party advertising for the app. Where information is processed by Apple services that are necessary to provide HotSwap features, those services are expected to provide protections consistent with their applicable privacy and security obligations.

6. Tracking

HotSwap is not designed to track you across third-party apps or websites for advertising purposes.

7. Retention

HotSwap retains or processes information only as needed to provide the relevant functionality, subject to the lifecycle of Shared Sessions, local app state and the operation of Apple's CloudKit services. Hosts can end Shared Sessions from within HotSwap.

8. Deletion and privacy choices

You can revoke camera or notification permissions in iOS Settings. You can end or leave Shared Sessions in HotSwap where the relevant controls are available.

To request deletion of personal information associated with HotSwap, or for another privacy request, email support.hotswap@gmail.com. Include enough information for us to understand the request, but do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials.

See the dedicated Privacy Choices & Data Deletion page for more information.

9. Security

HotSwap uses platform services and security features provided by Apple for supported synchronization and app functionality. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children

HotSwap is a production utility and is not specifically directed to children.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated if HotSwap's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL with an updated date.
