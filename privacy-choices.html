Privacy Choices & Data Deletion

How to control permissions and request deletion of information associated with HotSwap.

You do not need to create a separate HotSwap account to use the app.

Notification permission

You can enable or disable HotSwap notifications at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → HotSwap.

Camera permission

You can enable or disable camera access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Camera access is only needed for scanning Shared Session QR codes.

Shared Sessions

Where available in the app, you can leave a Shared Session. Hosts can end their Shared Sessions. Ending or leaving a session stops HotSwap from continuing to use that session as an active session on the device.

Request deletion

To request deletion of personal information associated with your use of HotSwap, send an email to:

support.hotswap@gmail.com

Use the subject HotSwap Data Deletion Request and briefly describe what you want deleted. We may need enough information to identify the relevant Shared Session information or support correspondence. Do not send your Apple ID password or other account passwords.

Other privacy requests

You can also use the same email address to ask a privacy question, withdraw consent where applicable, or request information about HotSwap's handling of your data.
